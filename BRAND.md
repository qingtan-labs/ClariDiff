# ClariDiff Brand Notes

## Name

**ClariDiff**

Chinese product name: **明析**

The name combines “clarity” with “diff”: make complex changes understandable and expose what actually matters.

## Positioning

**Compare anything. See what matters.**

Chinese: **比较一切，只看真正重要的变化。**

Pronunciation: **CLAIR-ee-diff** (`/ˈklær.i.dɪf/`).

## Icon concept

Two overlapping documents make comparison immediately recognizable across data, source code, and prose. Their aligned horizontal marks represent comparable content; one warm amber change marker identifies the meaningful difference. Cool cyan and teal represent the two inputs. The symbol is text-free, language-independent, and avoids limiting the product to JSON or to a red-versus-green line diff.
